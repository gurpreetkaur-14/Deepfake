## Brand & Style

The visual identity is anchored in high-assurance cybersecurity and enterprise forensic intelligence. Built for rigorous analysis environments, the interface projects analytical precision, immutability, and calm under operational velocity. Rather than relying on hyperbolic decorative elements, it communicates reliability through disciplined contrast, tight spatial governance, and razor-sharp data hierarchy.

The design movement combines **Corporate Modern** structure with **Technical Telemetry**:
- Grounded slate-dark backdrops that eliminate visual fatigue during sustained investigative workflows.
- High-precision telemetry cards with subtle boundary delineation to structure deepfake risk vectors.
- Calculated, intentional bursts of primary cyan and state-specific alerts (forensic green for authentic verification, surgical crimson for synthetic/manipulated signatures).
- Systematic typography utilizing utilitarian Inter scales paired with monospace micro-telemetry for confidence metrics and cryptographic hashes.

## Colors

The palette establishes an authoritative dark telemetry space where luminance carries functional meaning:

- **Primary (`#06B6D4`)**: High-visibility cyan reserved for actionable elements, active telemetry scans, primary state triggers, and selected analytical filters.
- **Secondary (`#3B82F6`)**: Deep electric blue applied to navigational anchors, systemic infrastructure metrics, and secondary analytical models.
- **Tertiary (`#10B981`)**: Diagnostic green signifying authenticated biological signatures, pristine media states, and validated hash matches.
- **Alert / Destructive (`#F43F5E`)**: High-contrast rose-crimson indicating confirmed synthetic manipulation, facial warping artifacts, and anomaly detection breaches.
- **Neutral Core (`#0B0F19`)**: Base background canvas, supported by tiered container levels:
  - Base canvas: `#0B0F19`
  - Subsurface / Sidebar: `#111827`
  - Elevated inspection cards: `#1E293B`
  - Muted borders & dividers: `#334155` at low opacities

## Typography

Typography prioritizes dense, unambiguous scanning across high-information displays:

- **Hierarchy & Tracking**: Headings leverage tight negative tracking (`-0.025em` to `-0.01em`) to create dense, unified titles that anchor analytical sections. Labels and operational states use uppercase rendering with positive tracking (`0.04em` to `0.05em`) to preserve legibility at micro scales.
- **Numeric & Metric Display**: Tabular numbers (`tnum`) should be enabled across all telemetry grids, risk confidence gauges, and frame timestamps to eliminate visual jitter during real-time ML inference.
- **Responsiveness**: Large headers scale down gracefully on mobile viewport tiers, maintaining strict horizontal containment within single-column forensic reports.

## Layout & Spacing

The structural framework is built on a 12-column desktop-first fluid grid optimized for high-density side-by-side video frame inspection, frequency spectra, and prediction metrics:

- **Desktop (1200px+)**: 12 columns with `gutter-lg` (24px) and `margin-lg` (40px). Side navigation remains fixed at 260px, leaving the analytical stage flexible for media viewports and sidecar forensic inspect panels.
- **Tablet (768px - 1199px)**: 8 columns with `gutter` (20px) and `margin` (24px). Lateral inspectors collapse into a bottom tabbed drawer or stacked modular panels.
- **Mobile (<768px)**: 4 columns with `gutter-sm` (12px) and `margin-sm` (16px). Inspector and media views stack linearly; metrics collapse into unified full-width card modules.
- **Spacing Rhythm**: Spacing strictly derives from an 8pt base system (`space-xs` = 4px, `space-sm` = 8px, `space-md` = 16px, `space-lg` = 24px, `space-xl` = 32px) to ensure rigorous vertical alignment across tabular telemetry feeds.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses tonal surface layering combined with low-contrast structural boundaries rather than diffuse drop shadows:

- **Level 0 (Canvas Base)**: `#0B0F19` — the primary bedrock upon which all panels sit.
- **Level 1 (Subsurface / Navigation Panels)**: `#111827` with a 1px solid border (`rgba(51, 65, 85, 0.4)`).
- **Level 2 (Telemetry & Analysis Cards)**: `#1E293B` featuring a subtle top border highlight (`rgba(6, 182, 212, 0.15)`) and perimeter outline (`rgba(51, 65, 85, 0.6)`).
- **Level 3 (Modals, Contextual Drawers, Floating Inspector Tools)**: `#1E293B` overlaying a `#0B0F19` backdrop with 70% opacity and 8px blur. Elevated with a focused, directional ambient shadow: `0 8px 24px -4px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.6)`.
- **Active / Focus Illumination**: Threat alerts and active scan focus lines leverage an inner or localized rim glow: `0 0 12px rgba(6, 182, 212, 0.25)` for primary status and `0 0 12px rgba(244, 63, 94, 0.3)` for deepfake detection flags.

## Shapes

The design uses a restrained, disciplined corner treatment (`roundedness: 1` — Soft):

- Standard components (buttons, input fields, badges, telemetry cells) use a standard `4px` (`0.25rem`) radius.
- Cards, modal containers, and video canvas frames scale to `rounded-lg` (`8px` / `0.5rem`).
- Diagnostic chips and micro tags use `4px` to avoid friendly "pill" aesthetics, preserving the sharp precision of a defense-grade software interface.
- Circular geometry (`rounded-full`) is strictly restricted to radial metric rings, avatar anchors, and active signal status indicator dots.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary**: Solid cyan (`#06B6D4`) background with stark black/slate text (`#0B0F19`), font weight 600, 4px corner radius. Hover triggers slight brightness increase (`#22D3EE`) and a disciplined cyan rim glow.
- **Secondary / Ghost**: Transparent fill with 1px border (`#334155`), slate-200 text. Hover transitions border to cyan (`#06B6D4`) and background to `rgba(6, 182, 212, 0.08)`.
- **Destructive / Flagged**: Crimson background (`#F43F5E`) or transparent with `#F43F5E` border for instant anomaly containment actions.

### Forensic Telemetry Cards
- Slate background (`#1E293B`), 8px radius, bordered by 1px `rgba(51, 65, 85, 0.6)`.
- Header zone contains uppercase tracking labels (`label-sm`), confidence score badges, and action menus separated by a 1px baseline rule (`#334155`).

### Status Badges & Chips
- Compact, rectangular with 4px border radius.
- **Authentic**: Emerald green tint (`rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.12)`), solid text (`#10B981`), 1px green border (`rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.3)`).
- **Synthetic / Anomaly**: Rose tint (`rgba(244, 63, 94, 0.12)`), text (`#F43F5E`), 1px rose border (`rgba(244, 63, 94, 0.4)`).
- **Processing**: Cyan tint (`rgba(6, 182, 212, 0.12)`), text (`#06B6D4`), accompanied by a pulsing status pip.

### Inputs & Dropzones
- **Form Controls**: Slate foundation (`#111827`), 1px `#334155` border, inset text. On focus: border shifts directly to `#06B6D4` with an inner 1px sharp cyan focus ring.
- **Media Ingestion Dropzone**: Dashed 2px border (`#334155`), background `#111827`. When dragging assets over: border accelerates to animated cyan dash (`#06B6D4`) with surface shift to `rgba(6, 182, 212, 0.04)`.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- Square (`4px` radius) or circular elements with `#111827` fill and `#475569` border. Selected state fills with `#06B6D4` with a high-contrast dark checkmark.

### Video Frame Anomaly Scrubbers & Timeline
- High-density timeline bar with integrated heatmaps indicating frame-by-frame synthetic probability. Red markers pinpoint flagged landmark mismatches; cyan scrubber head indicates current playhead position.
